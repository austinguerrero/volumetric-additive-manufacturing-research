# Volumetric Additive Manufacturing (VAM)
**Undergraduate research · Valparaiso University with UC Berkeley · Aug 2026 – Present**

Research into volumetric 3D printing, where light projected into a rotating vial of photopolymer resin cures a whole part at once.

## What I did

- Working on the implementation of ultrasonic transducers in the VAM system.
- Investigating photopolymerization-based volumetric 3D printing.
- Supporting experimental design and process optimization to improve print quality and efficiency.

## Tools
Ultrasonic transducers, photopolymer resins, experimental design, process optimization
